# Belvedere — current design

This file holds the current design and the reasons behind non-obvious choices. Edit sections in place when the design changes; do not append history. Exact values live in code; numbers here are the ones Nick chose deliberately.

## Conventions
- Slot index = row*3 + col; row 0 = north, col 0 = west; slot 4 = center.
- Room id = home slot: 0 Bedroom, 1 Bathroom, 2 Study, 3 Kitchen, 5 Living, 6 Dining, 7 Foyer, 8 Laundry; -1 = empty slot (gap).
- Directions N=0 E=1 S=2 W=3. A room side is a doorway if it is interior in the solved layout, otherwise a window. Foyer south is the front door and blocks walking like a window.

## Board and rules
- 3×3 grid, eight rooms, one gap. Rooms never rotate. 6 m interiors, 0.25 m walls (two 0.125 m halves, one per room, so faces never coincide), 6.25 m slot pitch, 3 m ceilings, eye height 1.65 m, 3 m/s walking.
- Glazed double doors open automatically when the facing side is the gap or another room's doorway; otherwise they stay shut and both leaves rattle on use. Windows never allow walking, but slides are legal across any side.
- Lever: hold E or left mouse on it, push mouse/WASD (view-relative, converted to world axes), release. Into the gap = the whole room slides with the player riding; otherwise a wooden clunk. Slide = 1.6 s (0.09 s take-up, 1.43 s travel with half-second braking, 0.08 s docking), no overshoot or shake; input is locked and board state commits once on docking. Doors close in transit and re-evaluate on landing.
- The gap is dark concrete with flush crossing tracks, recessed 2 cm to avoid coplanar faces with passing rooms. When the gap is on the perimeter you can walk out onto the lawn and back in through it. Exterior doors stay blocked.
- Every room carries its furniture, fixtures, clues and floor patterns when it slides.

## Starts and modes
- Default launch = seed A opening `[6,2,5,0,1,3,7,8,-1]`: Laundry starts bottom-center, with the gap to its right. The game opens on the Bedroom wall title. Classic seed A retains its original arrangement; other seeds keep their original spawns, 1.7 m behind the lever facing the gap.
- `--seed=B|C|random|<index>` picks from `data/accepted_14_20.json`: 1,816 states that are 14–20 optimal slides from solved, with the player's room having a doorway open to the gap. The spec's random-walk scramble was replaced because simulation (`tools/sim/`) showed random walks barely leave solved. Only 7% of boards are reachable; there are no dead states.
- `--classic` = the original single-goal jam puzzle with a perimeter barrier (random accepted start unless `--seed`). `--courtyard` = archived six-room/three-gap experiment. Both keep their tests; don't extend them.
- `--invert-y`, `--web-entry` (exercises the web play-gesture path natively).

## Rooms and furnishings
Accent colours: Bedroom dusty blue, Bathroom sea-glass, Study deep green, Kitchen mustard, Living terracotta, Dining plum, Foyer cream/wood with a checker floor, Laundry slate blue. The accent goes on one wall and on the door casings, so a room can be identified through any neighbour's glass.
- Static, original low-poly furniture with simple colliders. Keep a 1.2 m ring clear around each lever and clear approaches to all four sides. Exceptions: Bedroom (the bed and 4 m-wide rug are centered between the two north windows, with the bedside table on the west side; the lever is approached from the east, south or west, and the north windows via the west aisle) and Dining (a 2.3 m table 1.55 m west of the lever, with an aisle around both ends). Tall storage stays on solid walls; window sightlines stay clear.
- Foyer: trophy cabinet NW, bench NE, coat and umbrella stands SE, console/mirror SW. Study safe under the east windows between the globe and reading chair. Bathroom: tub NW, washstand and mirror SW, a 1.5 m bench beneath the NE window turned 12° toward the room with its towel rail on the east wall, and a 1.5 m linen cupboard centered on the SE south-wall section. The tub is pulled out of its NW corner and turned 8° into the room.
- Wall art: one small wood-framed, wordless print each in Bathroom (sea-glass waves above the linen cupboard), Study (muted green botanical above the reading chair), Kitchen (ochre landscape between the west windows), Living (terracotta arch above the fireplace), and Dining (plum vase above the sideboard). Original SVGs in `assets/wall_art/`, rendered as printed paper; no lights, animation, interaction, or collision. Bedroom, Foyer, and Laundry keep their existing wall pieces.
- House plan: a framed wordless floor plan (`assets/house_plan.svg`, from `tools/gen_plan.py`) hangs between the Laundry's east windows, in line with its west door, the lever and the first blue lamp. It teaches the core rule: every outer side has two windows, every inner side a double door, and the solid front door with its step is the only asymmetry. No room names, colours or numbers, so it gives the rule and not the answer. It is drawn facing the wall (east up, south right), like the Bedroom grid, so the Laundry is its top-right square. White paper and blue ink use the shared toon shader's `printed` mode. If it proves too small, the fallback is an inspect zoom.
- Decorative lamps and the fireplace stay unlit, so only objective fixtures signal game state. Mirrors are painted highlights, not reflections.
- Budgets: ≤60 furniture/trim meshes in Bedroom and ≤90 in other rooms.

## Objectives, coins, trophy
- Seven objective rooms (all except Foyer) award one blue Bel coin each, in sequence. The next target is the unfinished room with the fewest legal slides from the current layout (forward 0–1 BFS: slides cost 1, walks 0), computed only when a target activates or completes. Rooms already home are credited during selection. Completion is permanent and never locks a room in place.
- Seed A: Laundry is the first blue target, one slide from home. Sliding it east from bottom-center is the only slide reachable from the opening rooms; entering it alone earns nothing. Docking at home triggers a one-time guided look: the camera eases to the blue lamp, holds, shows the lamp turning warm, then presents the coin. Later targets never take the camera.
- Lights: the target's pendant is steady blue `#6EBFFF` with a blue-tinted shade. Other objective rooms that are home are warm white `#FFF0DA`. Everything else is off. The seven Foyer chandelier lights show which objective rooms are currently home, not coins.
- Rewards (coins, binoculars, stopwatch) fly to a presentation in front of the camera and stay until any fresh key, mouse button or gamepad button is pressed. That press is consumed. Presses made while the reward is still arriving are swallowed. Mouse motion and the wheel never accept. Each queued reward needs its own press. Presentations wait during pause, slides, recovery and item inspection. The large coin has a stamped bell emblem and appears on the left beside “Token” and “Collect them all”, using the same Fondamento typography and caption layout as binocular rewards. Captions hide while a presentation is suspended and fade as the item enters inventory. The HUD coin is unlettered and it and its count hide while the wallet is empty, including after deposits; nothing glows.
- The Foyer cabinet takes one coin per fresh E/click, with a visible insertion and recorded clink. Its readout shows only the number of coins still needed. The trophy has a hollow gold cup shaped like an upside-down bell, with a flared lip, slim curved side handles, slim stem, and dark plinth. The seventh deposit releases the trophy and opens Thanks for Playing (Keep Playing / Quit to Menu). Keep Playing retains all state.

## Assembled-house ending (independent of coins)
All eight rooms home opens the red front door (3.5 s; it blocks until fully open), lights its house emblem and awards any outstanding coins. Crossing the doorway eases the camera 20 s along a red carpet under a pastel rainbow. Plain white Fondamento text without outlines: "Belvedere" (1.8 m high, fades in over the first 10 s); on arrival "A game by Nick Schaefer" (4 s fade), a 3 s wait, then "Thank you for playing" (4 s fade). The lower two lines sit closer to the camera so the three lines have equal visible gaps. One second after the thank-you is fully visible, run totals at bottom left and “Any key to continue” at bottom right fade in over 1.4 s. The ending then waits indefinitely for a fresh keyboard press before fading back to the main menu; held keys, mouse clicks/motion, and presses before the results finish appearing cannot dismiss it. Pause hides the results and freezes the sequence; Resume continues it. Once the results are ready, any fresh key (including Escape/P) continues instead of pausing. Stemless flowers sprout every 0.15 s, only inside the camera frustum, 2.5–12 m ahead, from a 160-instance MultiMesh without shadows.

Run tracking is local to the current playthrough and resets only for a new game. Shifts count every completed room move, including reversals, and accumulate across bed resets. Sleeps count confirmed bed sleeps plus fall recoveries once each; the total has a trailing asterisk if any were falls. Cancelling sleep and blocked shifts do not count. Time starts when play begins, pauses for every button menu (including settings, controls, sleep confirmation, and the trophy screen), and stops when the rainbow sequence begins. Slides, item inspection, sleep transitions, and falls remain part of active time. Results use “Shifts”, “Sleeps”, and “Time” with minutes:seconds (hours when needed); no tracking counters appear during ordinary play. `tests/run_stats_tests.gd` verifies accounting and menu timing; `tools/capture.sh objectives A screenshots/ending --ending-only` captures the ending and results using staged totals.

## Study safe and fruit clues
- Four plain buttons count fresh taps independently and in any order: red 3, yellow 2, orange 4, green 1. Turning the handle checks the counts. A wrong attempt rattles and clears all taps; a correct one opens the door and presents the binoculars. No digits, tallies or labels. Safe state survives slides and falls.
- Clues: 3 apples on the Kitchen prep table, 2 separate bananas on the lower shelf of Dining's china cabinet, 4 oranges on Living's 0.90 m sideboard, 1 pear on the Bedroom dresser. Each clue renders only on pixels whose camera ray enters its own room through a window or a fully closed glass door. It is never visible indoors, through open or moving doors, or from above. No outline or shadow pass, which would leak it. Fruit stays out of static batches so its mask follows slides.

## Binoculars
B raises them along one arc, cuts through dark to a north-up orthographic overview of the live manor, and B or Escape returns to the exact first-person view; P still pauses. Escape also cancels raising and consumes repeated presses while lowering. Overview only: room-coloured roofs replace ceilings, carrying home-order numbers 1 2 3 / 4 · 5 / 6 7 8 that move with the rooms. Door lintels are hidden. Window-wall tops get black 0.34 m borders; the Foyer’s front-door edge gets a matching full-width red border, using the door’s red. Ordinary doorways get none. A small cream head-and-shoulders silhouette marks the occupied room in its southeast roof corner, clear of the number; in the gap or on the lawn it marks the player’s position. Shadows stay on. Movement, interaction and slides are locked while viewing. The player starts without them; opening the Study safe is the only way to earn them. The reward presents the binoculars on the left with large “Binoculars” lettering and “Press B to use” alongside, in Fondamento. The caption fades with the presentation and hides during pause.

## Stopwatch and foundation hatch
A fixed, shallow metal hatch sits in the northwest foundation square, initially beneath Dining. It becomes visible only when that square is empty, and is hidden with its interaction colliders disabled during slides. Its brass pull opens the hinged lid and automatically awards the watch. The watch is revealed inside as the lid opens, rests briefly, then lifts vertically out of the recess and arcs into its reward presentation; no second pickup interaction. Movement and interaction stay locked through opening and presentation. No key or additional code. The recess retains the solid walking floor. Once the watch clears the recess, the lid gently closes on its own with a quiet latch sound. The empty hatch is no longer interactive, and stays closed and empty when rooms cover and uncover it. Pause suspends both opening and closing. From the default opening, the first normally walkable access takes ten slides.

The original brass stopwatch has an ivory face and a centered number-only readout: the exact minimum room slides needed to assemble the house from the current board and player component. Walks cost zero; the lawn is equivalent to the sole gap. Deliberate fall/respawn shortcuts are excluded. The reverse 0–1 BFS builds incrementally within a small per-frame budget; the watch only displays completed distances, never provisional estimates. The count refreshes after committed slides and occupancy changes, reaches zero when assembled, and does not count tokens or elapsed time.

The reward uses the same fresh-press acceptance as other items, with “Stopwatch”, “Shows the fewest shifts needed for assembly.” and “Press T to use”. T raises/lowers the owned watch at the lower-right edge, lifting from below with a slight wrist-like turn but no visible hand. Escape also lowers it, including during the raise. P pauses it. Starting to walk automatically lowers the watch, with movement and looking available immediately during lowering; interactions and binocular use wait until it is stowed. Walking does not dismiss binoculars. Accepting the reward sends it into a small stopwatch icon beside the coin HUD and, when owned, binoculars. The inventory icon appears only after acquisition and carries no count; raise the watch to read its numerical hint. Pause hides and suspends its presentation/inspection; returning to title or falling clears inspection while preserving ownership. Controls lists Stopwatch — T only while owned.

`tools/try_stopwatch.sh` opens a playable ten-slide fixture on the lawn beside the unopened hatch, with the legal route's token progress retained and the stopwatch still uncollected. `tools/capture.sh stopwatch A screenshots/stopwatch` verifies and captures discovery, collection, use and Controls. `tests/stopwatch_tests.gd` covers the real interaction rays, state retention, input lifecycle and exact counts.

## Exterior and fall
- A meadow with low hills. 15 near trees, eight mixed broadleaf/pine groves plus four lone trees, and a hazy 88–104 m treeline with a gap behind the rainbow. All are composed for views from the house. Keep the Bedroom window tree at (-17, -6.9), the current minimum clearance from the house, and an open south carpet approach.
- Walking off the meadow edge gives a ~15 s fall past a flat dark casing whose underside carries a large unlit QR code (https://puzzaholics.com/belvedere-assembly, error correction Q, `tools/gen_qr.sh`). The QR fills an exact opening in the casing, not a coplanar backing, which flickered. Then the screen fades and the player wakes near the foot of the bed in the Bedroom's current position with all progress kept.

## Title, pause, menus
- Sleeping: the Bedroom bed becomes interactable after the first completed room shift. E or left click opens “Start over?” with the smaller label “Sleep to reset the rooms. Keep your tokens and items.” and Sleep / Cancel, initially focusing Cancel. Cancel or a pause key leaves the layout untouched. Sleep fades out, restores the exact initial room arrangement (including random starts), and fades in beside the bed without returning to the title. Collected tokens, cabinet deposits, completed token objectives, owned items, and safe/hatch progress remain unchanged. The next blue target is chosen from uncollected rooms, without awarding tokens during the reset. The room-slide count resets; the bed waits for another shift before becoming active again. Falling still preserves the room arrangement. `tools/capture.sh sleep A screenshots/sleep` verifies the confirmation, transition, and retained inventory; `tests/bed_sleep_tests.gd` covers activation, cancellation, preservation, and reset geometry.
- The seed A title is painted on the Bedroom's west wall: large "Belvedere" and "Play" in ink, with no backdrop. Both fade over 0.55 s when Play captures the pointer; control returns at the same view. The 3D scene fades in over 1.4 s on launch while the menu stays visible.
- The permanent wall fixture is a spaced 3×3 grid of warm-gray squares. The target room's current board slot is light blue. The grid shows west at the top and north to the right, matching the view of this wall. It updates on target changes and slides, rides with the Bedroom, and shows no blue once all objectives are done.
- Escape or P pauses (Controls shows "Esc / P"), and the same key resumes from the pause menu, not from Settings or Controls. Reward presentations never accept either key. Web exception: Escape does not resume, because browsers never grant pointer lock from Escape (it is not a user-activation key) and the menu would bounce back; P and Resume do.
- Web: losing pointer lock during play opens the pause menu, because browsers end the lock themselves (Escape, tab switches, clicks outside the canvas) without passing Escape to the game. Lock requests resolve after the click, so only a granted lock that ends, or a request still unmet after 1 s, pauses. During binocular or stopwatch use, losing the lock lowers the item instead; a subsequent click recaptures the pointer without opening pause. Slides and falls finish first, matching Escape. `tests/pointer_tests.gd` covers this with `--web-entry`.
- Title and pause have Play/Resume, Controls, Settings and Sound: On/Off. Controls lists Move, Look, Interact, Pull lever and Pause, plus Binoculars — B and Stopwatch — T only while owned. Settings: FOV 60–100, sensitivity, invert-Y, master/effects/music volume, persisted in `user://settings.cfg`.
- Buttons are plain lettering: hover/focus tints the word and pressing lowers it 2 px. Focus tints appear only after keyboard input, and hover never moves focus (Enter after hovering Quit must not quit).

## Audio
- Recorded CC0 foley shaped by `tools/gen_audio.sh`, kept at natural speed (pitch-stretching sounded synthetic). Three footstep sounds every 1.05 m: soft (wood rooms, rugs, carpet), normal (tile rooms, gap) and grass (quiet). Lever grab/return clicks and a blocked clunk. One 2 s slide sound that lands on docking; music ducks 6 dB. Only the nearest opening and nearest closing door are voiced. Locked-door rattle = handle catch plus one push. Coin acquisition and insertion clinks. The foundation hatch reuses a quiet opening creak and closing latch, and the stopwatch leaving its recess uses a soft existing mechanical click; the watch does not tick.
- A distant bird loop plays only outdoors (gap or lawn), very quiet (−33 dB). Menu presses (never hover) play one brass latch click (`ManorAudio.MENU_CLICK`), deferred so muting is silent and unmuting is audible.
- Music: replaceable loop `assets/audio/manor_theme.ogg` (an original felt-piano loop) at 35%. It starts after 3 s and fades in over 5 s, multiplied by slide ducking.
- Web: the Effects and Music buses must exist in `default_bus_layout.tres`, because non-threaded web audio ignores buses added at runtime. Music fade follows the heard playback position because browsers hold audio until the first input. Headless runs skip playback but emit `ManorAudio.cued` for tests.

## Art and rendering
- Late-afternoon warm light, cream plaster, warm wood, one strong accent per room, chunky readable furniture and little clutter. Flat-shaded low poly, original geometry generated in code; no downloaded models or textures. No reference images are stored.
- Godot 4.7 Compatibility renderer, so web matches desktop. One shared toon shader (`shaders/toon.gdshader`): 3-band ramp, room shadow tint and a faint paper grain. Inverted-hull warm-brown ink outlines, never black. The grain, outline wobble, planks and tiles are sampled in mesh-local space, because world-space sampling made surfaces swim during slides. No post-processing, bloom or fog.
- Exterior: every room wall's outward face (mesh −Z; interior trims sit on +Z) is painted by the toon shader as warm-grey lap siding over a 0.3 m charcoal base, one colour for the whole house; no added geometry. Courses are 0.18 m with a lap shadow line. They use world height, which slides never change, so they line up across wall pieces and rooms. Lines widen to at least a pixel and lighten to match, so distant walls don't alias. East/west walls' outer end faces are house corners and get siding (`siding_corners`). Other end and reveal faces (door jambs, window reveals) are cream casings. A window pressed against a neighbour shows that room's siding. `tools/capture.sh exterior` checks facades, the gap, corners and interior windows.
- Sun `#FFD9A8`, pitch −28°, yaw 235°, 2 PSSM splits, 40 m shadows; sun patches on floors are a key image. Wall corners are butt-jointed and trims sit on their owner's interior side: no coplanar opaque faces with different colours (tested). No hull expansion on adjoining wall planes or thin door joinery, which caused detached ink strips.

## Performance
Most cost is the sun's shadow pass. Batched outline shells are separate non-casting geometry. Furniture the fixed sun can never reach through its own room's openings casts no shadow (`scripts/manor_sun_reach.gd`, ray-checked by `tests/shadow_tests.gd`). Repeated opaque pieces are batched per rigid assembly and room shell; glass stays separate. Foliage is unshaded vertex colour, merged into one mesh per 32 m cell or treeline sector; only trees within 46 m cast shadows. Because unshaded foliage can't darken, shrubs and casting grove trees sit outside tree and house shadows. MultiMesh batches keep custom bounds. Godot's dummy renderer doesn't store MultiMesh transforms, so headless geometry checks keep their own. Untried next steps: per-material merging of room singletons, and portal culling. Measure with `tools/profile.sh`.

## Rejected — don't reintroduce
Camera shake (and its setting); pulsing, scaling or animated objective indicators; floor targets, arrows and check marks; a target diagram in the overview; completion chimes; outdoor wind, per-room ambient loops and a front-door creak; a separate rug footstep; tonal or bright hard-floor steps; birds indoors; digits or tally marks on the safe; menu hover pills, a circular Play icon and ornamental diamonds; a smiley meadow underside; billboard or pancake trees and evenly scattered trees; ordinary doorway borders in the overview; lit decorative lamps; per-room exterior siding tints.
