# Belvedere

Follow Nick's global guidelines in `/Users/nick/.codex/AGENTS.md`.

First-person 3D sliding-room puzzle. Godot 4.7, Compatibility renderer. Spec: `docs/SPEC.md`; amendments and decisions: `docs/DECISIONS.md`; art: `docs/STYLE.md`.

Commands (kept in `tools/`; update this list when scripts change):
- Run: `tools/run.sh`
- Rule + solver tests (headless): `tools/test.sh`
- Classic-mode fun-test bots + geometry probes (headless): `tools/fun_test.sh` → `docs/FUNTEST.md`
- Screenshots: `tools/capture.sh [title|coins|safe|binoculars|fall|trees|furnishings|stage3|objectives|single-gap|courtyard|solve|review] [seed] [out_dir] [extra flags]`
  Furnishing captures accept `--rooms=5,7` to inspect selected rooms.
- QR flicker regression at falling distances (real window): `tools/check_qr.sh`
- Door-frame close-up pixel check (real window): `tools/check_doors.sh [out_dir]`
- Render submission benchmark (real window): `tools/profile.sh [output_json]`
- Web build (local zip, never uploaded): `tools/build_itch.sh`
- Generate/verify the underside QR: `tools/gen_qr.sh` (macOS; or pass screenshot paths to verify their QR destination).
- Read-only export scope/identity preflight: `python3 tools/check_export_identity.py`
- Regenerate `assets/audio/` from the CC0 sources in `tools/audio_sources/` (needs ffmpeg + oggenc): `tools/gen_audio.sh`

Controls: WASD/mouse; hold left mouse or E on the lever, push mouse/WASD, release. Default seed A opens on the Bedroom wall title; Play captures the pointer and starts from that same viewpoint. Escape releases it and shows the pause play icon. `tools/run.sh` starts eight rooms and one gap on the 3×3 grid, using seed A with lawn access through the gap. The permanent Bedroom wall fixture is a spaced 3×3 grid: nine squares in one textured warm-gray color, with the target room’s current board position light blue (initially lower-left). The wall grid has west at the top to match the viewing direction; the marker updates when the target changes or its room moves. The large wall title and Play button fade out together over 0.55 seconds when starting; no title backdrop. Alternate seeds and legacy modes retain their original spawn positions. Seven room objectives award blue Bel coins, each presented with a solid stamped bell emblem before entering the HUD wallet. The small HUD coin is unlettered; coins have no glow or halo. Start with no coins: the already-home Laundry is the first blue target in seed A, and entering it starts a one-time guided look: gently face the blue lamp, hold, show it turn warm, then present the opening coin and return control once it reaches the wallet. Escape pauses the sequence. E or left mouse inserts one coin into the Foyer cabinet; seven deposits release the trophy and a Thanks for Playing screen (Keep Playing / Quit to Menu). Blue light and a subtly blue shade mark the target room (including the already-home opening target); warm room/Foyer lights track current placement. Earned coins remain owned when rooms move. Placing all eight rooms home still unlocks the separate front-door ending. The meadow has a flat dark casing with a large white-backed QR underneath linking to https://puzzaholics.com/belvedere-assembly; it replaces the smiley. Walking off the meadow gives a fifteen-second fall, then a fade back to the current Bedroom bedside with progress retained. `--seed=B|C|random|<index>` selects other original starts. `--courtyard` retains the earlier six-room/three-gap experiment. `tools/run.sh --classic --seed=A` selects the old eight-room jam layout (`--classic` alone selects a random accepted state); `--invert-y` inverts mouse Y.

The Study safe sits below the east windows between the globe stand and reading chair. Its four plain colored buttons count fresh E/left-mouse taps independently: red ×3, yellow ×2, orange ×4, green ×1, in any order. Turn the handle to check; a wrong attempt rattles and clears all taps, and a correct attempt opens the safe and automatically presents its binocular reward. No numbers, tally dots or labels appear on the safe. Fruit clues are 3 red apples on Kitchen’s preparation table, 2 separate yellow bananas on the lower shelf of Dining’s china cabinet (upper bowls retained), 4 oranges on Living’s sideboard, and 1 green pear on Bedroom’s dresser. They appear only through their room’s windows, never indoors or through doors, and move with their rooms. Safe input/open state and binocular ownership survive room movement and falls. `tools/capture.sh safe A review/safe` checks rendered visibility and native input.

Binoculars: B raises them to the eyes, transitions to a centered north-up orthographic overview, and B again lowers them to the exact first-person view. Normal shadows stay on. Overview replaces ceilings with room-colored roofs carrying fixed home-order numbers 1–8 (1 2 3 / 4 gap 5 / 6 7 8), which move with the rooms. There is no target diagram. Doorway lintels disappear only in overview; only window-wall tops have black borders. Doorway and front-door edges have no borders. A small cream dot marks the player. Movement, interactions and slides are disabled while viewing. Escape pauses the view/animation. Fruit clues remain hidden from above. Opening the safe automatically flies the binoculars out, presents them, and places them in the HUD without a pickup. This testing pass starts with binoculars already owned; `tools/run.sh --earn-binoculars` disables that temporary grant. `tools/capture.sh binoculars A review/binoculars` captures the animation, overview, return and reward.

Vegetation is composed for views from the house, not close inspection: 15 near trees keep their positions, 46 more stand in eight groves plus four lone trees, and a hazy low-detail treeline rings the meadow at 88–104 m with a gap behind the rainbow. Rounded broadleaf trees and layered pines share baked vertex colors that fade toward the far-band tint with distance. All foliage, including leafy shrubs, merges into one mesh per 32 m cell or treeline sector, with no outline passes, grain sampling or per-tree CPU updates. Only trees within 46 m of the house cast shadows. Unshaded foliage cannot darken in shadow, so shrubs and casting grove trees are placed outside tree and house shadows. Keep trunk collisions, the Bedroom window tree at (-17, -6.9), and the existing minimum house clearance. `tests/vegetation_tests.gd` checks layout, shadows, merging and geometry budgets.

Shadow-pass cost: batched outline shells are separate non-casting geometry, and furniture the fixed sun can never reach through its own room's windows or doorways casts no shadows (`scripts/manor_sun_reach.gd`). `tests/shadow_tests.gd` ray-checks every trimmed piece.

The macOS Unsigned Test preset is authorized only for local unsigned/unnotarized testing in Stage 8; run the identity preflight first.

Rules: the title screen may show “Belvedere”; the authorized additional text is the coin HUD count, cabinet number-only remaining-coins readout, and Thanks for Playing with Keep Playing / Quit to Menu. The binocular overview also permits the eight roof numbers. Otherwise no visible instruction text, labels, room names, counters or taglines. Title and pause have persistent sound on/off and settings icons. Settings cover FOV, sensitivity, invert-Y, and master/effects/music volumes. Audio is quiet and physical: footsteps, lever, doors, room slides, coin insertion, distant birds outdoors only (no wind, no room loops, no completion chime), and a replaceable music loop at `assets/audio/manor_theme.ogg`. All visible text uses bundled Fondamento Regular (SIL OFL 1.1). All other art and audio original or CC0 with entries in `docs/CREDITS.md`. Never push or publish.
